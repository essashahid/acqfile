# Competitor review: what the customer actually sees

Reviewed 20 September 2026. Sources are each company's own help centre, product pages and the product screenshots published there. I could not sign up and click through live accounts, so this is what they show publicly, which is still their real product.

## FileInvite (document collection for lenders, used for SBA 7(a))

- **Screens found:** the client portal home, a requested item opened, the list after uploading. Source: help.fileinvite.com, "Client Portal", "Completing, Signing, and Returning Requested Files", "Upload Files to a Request".
- **Navigation:** one page. A left rail with four icons: Requests, Contact, Help, Video. The portal opens on Requests.
- **Asking for documents:** a sentence from a named person ("Bonnie Roskes from Bonnie's Company has requested some information from you"), then a numbered list of items. Clicking an item opens it with instructions and an upload button. One finishing button: "I'm finished".
- **Progress and status:** "Total requests 5" and "Requests are due in 16 days". Each item shows "open", then a "SAVED" tag once something is uploaded.
- **Words:** Invite, Requests, Saved, I'm finished.
- **Problems:** staff approve or reject each returned document with feedback. A rejected item goes back to open.
- **Worth borrowing:** the request comes from a named human; a short numbered list; a messages panel beside the list; one clear finish action.
- **Avoid:** the dated look, tiny grey status text, a heavy icon rail for four links, the word "Requests" as the name of the page.

## Floify (mortgage point of sale)

- **Screens found:** the borrower's loan page with owed, pending and accepted counts; the milestone bar; the upload modal. Source: help.floify.com, "Portal Overview For Borrowers", "Review a Borrower's Milestone Updates", release notes of 19 August 2021.
- **Navigation:** one page per loan, titled "Your purchase". Category tabs (Assets, Income, Misc) only when the lender uses them.
- **Asking for documents:** a table: name, a full description of exactly what is acceptable ("All bank statements for all accounts from the last 2 months. Include all numbered pages"), and icon actions.
- **Progress and status:** three chips: **owed, pending, accepted**. Above them a row of milestone bubbles that fill in as the loan moves. A named "Your Contact".
- **Words:** Docs Owed, Docs Pending Review, Docs Accepted, needs list, loan flow.
- **Problems:** staff reject a document; the borrower is notified and it returns to owed.
- **Worth borrowing:** the three-state model is the single best idea in this review. Also the named contact, stages across the top, and the precise description of what to upload.
- **Avoid:** fourteen unlabeled bubbles, icon-only actions, a table, a red "Overdue 544 days" badge.

## LoanBud (SBA business acquisition financing, the closest rival)

- **Screens found:** the application flow and the broker's business page. Source: loanbud.io product images and the February 2026 launch announcement.
- **Navigation:** the application has a left rail of four named stages with a sub-label each (Basic Information, Business Information, Owner Information, Agreement). The broker's page has three tabs: Overview, Documents, Messages.
- **Asking for information:** one card in the middle of the page, one task at a time. "Step 3/6" with a segmented line. Headline "Tell Us a Bit More About You", then a reason: "This helps lenders get a full picture."
- **Progress and status:** named stages, not counts. The outcome is a single banner: "Prequalified. Your business is prequalified for an SBA loan until May 24, 2025" with one button, "Download letter".
- **Words:** warm and direct; every ask comes with a reason.
- **Problems:** a "Deal Readiness Report that flags gaps with clear next steps".
- **Worth borrowing:** named stages, one task per screen, a reason with every ask, one outcome sentence with one button, generous space and large type.
- **Noticeably better than the others:** LoanBud. It is the quality bar. FileInvite and Floify have the right structure but look ten years older.

## What none of them do

All three collect and track. None reads the documents and tells the person, at the moment of upload, that it is the wrong year or unsigned, and none notices that two documents disagree. That is this product's reason to exist, so it must show up in the customer's experience as help, not as a list of flags.

## The patterns we take

1. Three states the customer can hold in their head: To do, With us for review, Done. (Floify)
2. Four named stages in a left rail instead of counts and bars. (LoanBud)
3. One sentence that answers "what do I need to do", addressed by name, from a named contact. (FileInvite, LoanBud)
4. One task per screen, with why we ask and exactly what to upload. (LoanBud, Floify)
5. One outcome sentence and one button. (LoanBud)
6. No tabs, no tables, no badges on customer pages.
