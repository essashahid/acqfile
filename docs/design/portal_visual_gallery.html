Proposed direction for our redesign

These are design recommendations drawn from the references and the brief. They are not claims of measured usability or instructions to copy a competitor.

01

Home: tell me what to do next

Start with the current stage, a short set of actions, what the team is reviewing, and a named contact. Blend BL01 is the clearest whole-page reference in this set.

02

Progress: use meaningful stages

Use a few understandable stages, with task details below. Floify FL01 and FL05 are useful models. Do not confuse an upload count with application approval.

03

Documents: give context before upload

A requirement should explain the document, period, person or company, and acceptance conditions. Add “I already sent this” or a question route where our workflow can handle it.

04

Problems: give one clear repair

Replace a raw mismatch flag with what needs clarification, why, and the specific next action. CS01 and MX01 show repair choices. Preserve valid uploads.

05

Staff logic stays with staff

Internal checks, raw paths, identifiers, and reviewer decisions should not become the borrower’s navigation. CS05 is staff context, not a client design reference.

06

Prototype a complete loop

Test an initial visit, partial completion, return visit, upload, correction, resubmission, and waiting-for-review state. These are proposed acceptance scenarios, not claims about every competitor.

Evidence limits that matter

LoanBud: Public marketing pages and the application entry link were found. The application itself did not expose inspectable screens in the research browser. No substitute image is labelled as LoanBud.

Dates: An asset-folder date or a recently updated article does not establish the age of the running product. Legacy examples remain labelled as legacy.

Screen types: Help screenshots, published product screens, device-framed product views, and illustrative UI compositions are different kinds of evidence. None is a session in a live customer account.

TaxDome, Finmo, and File Request Pro: Workflow documentation was reviewed. Missing or inaccessible image assets were not replaced with invented screens.

Roles: The Content Snare rejection dialog is staff-side context. Staff pipelines and workflow builders were otherwise excluded from the core visual set.

Reuse: Images belong to their respective owners. These links are for reference and analysis, not a licence to reuse competitor branding or artwork in our product.

Delivery: Source-image hosting may change or block hotlinks. The captions and source links remain available offline, but the images require a connection.
