# Synthetic document templates

Every synthetic file in `fixtures/deals` and `fixtures/demo/generated` is drawn from a template modelled on the real document it stands in for (A98). This page records what each template is based on, what is genuine and what is modelled, and the rules a template must keep so extraction and evaluation behave exactly as authored.

## Official forms

Tax documents are printed on the official IRS page for the return's own tax year, as tax software prints a client copy: the form's artwork and text, values placed in its boxes, a client-copy header in the top margin, and the page reduced to 94% to make room for that header.

| Document             | Form                        | Revision               | Source                                                        |
| -------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal tax return  | Form 1040, page 1           | 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | irs.gov/pub/irs-prior (2022–2024), irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf (2025) |
| Business tax return  | Form 1120-S, page 1         | 2023, 2024, 2025       | as above                                                      |
| Tax filing extension | Form 4868, page 1 (voucher) | 2025                   | irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f4868.pdf                                 |
| Transcript request   | Form 4506-C, page 1         | Rev. 10-2022 (current) | irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f4506c.pdf                                |

IRS forms are public domain. `pnpm exec tsx scripts/prepare-irs-forms.ts` downloads them, keeps the one page used, removes the fillable fields, and writes `fixtures/forms/irs/layout.json`, which records each source URL, the SHA-256 of the downloaded original, and where each value sits. Positions are found by matching the form's printed labels and line numbers to its field boxes, so a new revision is handled by rerunning the script and reviewing the rendered page.

SBA Forms 1919 and 413 are unchanged: they were already the official blanks with AcroForm values (A36).

## What keeps a document synthetic

- Every page carries a visible `SYNTHETIC` mark; most carry a light `SYNTHETIC SPECIMEN` watermark. Identity documents say `SPECIMEN` and "not a valid identification document".
- Governments and institutions other than the IRS form artwork are fictional (State of Tazmervale, Zelmivar Bank). No real bank or company branding is used.
- Identifiers stay in the synthetic ranges the lint enforces: EINs `00-…`, SSNs `9xx-…`, phones `555-01xx`, email `@example.com`. Account and routing numbers print masked.
- IRS phone numbers are replaced with synthetic ones; the lint checks everything we author on an IRS page (Decision 55 exempts only the form's own boilerplate).

## Rules every template keeps

Extraction and evaluation read these documents exactly as they read real ones, so the authored outcome depends on these rules. `fixtures/lib/doc/quotes.ts` is the single source for what each document states.

1. **A stated value reads as its evidence quote.** A fact is drawn either as a label and value on one row, or inside a sentence printed whole. Truth locators and planted-fault quotes are built from the same function, so they cannot drift. Workbook values are their own numeric cells.
2. **Reading order is drawing order.** PDF text extraction follows the order text is drawn, and starts a new line when the baseline changes. Templates draw each row left to right, top to bottom, and draw the document's title first.
3. **The title is in the first eight extracted lines** and matches a cue in `src/lib/config/document-signatures.ts`, without also matching another type's cue.
4. **Index lines stand alone.** `Name:`, `Period:`, `Document date:`, `Account ending:` and `Signature: …; Date: …` each print on their own line; the intake reads party, period, dates and signature status from them.
5. **One page per document**, `Page 1 of 1` on each, as the fixture plans assume. Agreements and leases therefore show their first page and signature block, not the full instrument.
6. **Figures tie out.** Statements balance, returns total to the planned ordinary income, bank transactions reconcile to the stated ending balance, and aging buckets sum to their totals. Supporting figures are generated deterministically per document.

Check the rules end to end: `pnpm fixtures:generate --check` (byte-identical regeneration) and `pnpm fixtures:check` (oracles, text quotes, cues, lint).
