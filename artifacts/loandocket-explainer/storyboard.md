## Edits

- Sign-in, page loads, and the return trips are cut. Those trips are browser Back from the value page to the document page, then its "Back to the review item" link; and browser Back from the adviser deal page after "Send my answer".
- The adviser deal page that appears after sending an answer is cut. Its reminder links are never used.
- Every other cut follows the recorded order, with 0.35 s dissolves. The report's answer section holds its last frame for 0.4 s. No steps were skipped, so no "after the remaining checks" transition is needed.
- Every app shot comes from one take on one deal (VCS-2026-015). Nothing is reused from the first version's footage, because that footage used a direct-URL workaround and a deal whose package had no recorded answer.
