# LoanDocket explainer: storyboard

**Audience:** commercial lenders, acquisition-loan brokers and advisers seeing LoanDocket for the first time.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 and AAC, 85.3 s. The footage fills the whole frame. Each section opens with one brief introduction: a dark lower band (app ink `#14202B`) with the section name and one line, shown for 3.2 s from 0.2 s after the section's first cut. It then fades out completely, band included, and nothing else is drawn over the footage. No narration is shown as text in the picture, and the picture carries no disclosure line; the sample-data and prepared-readings explanation is on the How it works page and in the README.

## Edits

- Sign-in, page loads, and the return trips are cut. Those trips are browser Back from the value page to the document page, then its "Back to the review item" link; and browser Back from the adviser deal page after "Send my answer".
- The adviser deal page that appears after sending an answer is cut. Its reminder links are never used.
- The question page plays as one view: the heading and source values, the recorded scroll down to the choices, the selection of 1,050,000, the typed reason and the Send my answer click. The cuts to the saved confirmation (49.56 s), the reopened Earlier answers (51.54 s) and Follow-ups (53.36 s) are hard cuts. Cue n7b ("The reviewer can reopen the saved answer and see the reason.") is spoken over the saved confirmation, the View recorded answer click and Earlier answers, 49.7–52.7 s.
- The closing card is silent and lasts 4.5 s.
- Step 1 opens on the top of the Documents page (its first frame held for 0.77 s) and scrolls to Add documents under the introduction; the files are chosen just after the introduction has gone, as the narration names them. Step 3's answer shot starts on the radio choice, so the introduction has gone before the reason is typed.
- Every other cut follows the recorded order, with 0.35 s dissolves. The report's answer section holds its last frame for 0.4 s. No steps were skipped, so no "after the remaining checks" transition is needed.
- Every app shot comes from one take on one deal (VCS-2026-015). Nothing is reused from the first version's footage, because that footage used a direct-URL workaround and a deal whose package had no recorded answer.
