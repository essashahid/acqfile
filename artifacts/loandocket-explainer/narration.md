## Wording left out on purpose

The narration does not say the conflict is resolved or that the file is complete or ready. The documents still disagree, and the version was created while work is outstanding. It makes no claim about faster approval, approval odds, compliance or time saved. It does not describe live AI reading: the on-screen disclosure says "Document readings prepared for this demo".
